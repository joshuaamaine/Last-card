How to play

  • Everyone gets 7 cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card by color, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything.
  • Can't play? Tap the deck to draw. If the new card fits, you can play it or keep it.
  • Skip jumps the next player. Reverse flips the order (with two players, you go again). +2 makes the next player draw two and miss a turn.
  • Wild picks the color. Wild +4 picks the color and the next player draws four.
  • Down to two cards on your turn? Tap Last card! If someone forgets, tap their face to catch them for two cards.
  • With two players, Skip, Reverse, +2 and +4 all mean you go again.
  • First to empty their hand wins. Everyone heads back to the lobby, and the next round starts when everyone taps Ready. Wins show on your face.
