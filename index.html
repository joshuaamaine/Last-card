How to play

Last Card rules

  • Everyone gets 7 cards. On your turn, play one card that matches the top card by color, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything.
  • Can't play, or don't want to? Draw one. If it fits, you can play it right away or keep it.
  • Skip jumps the next player. Reverse flips the order; with two players it means you go again. +2 makes the next player draw two and miss their turn.
  • Wild lets you pick the color. Wild +4 picks the color and makes the next player draw four. It can be played any time here.
  • Down to two cards on your turn? Tap Last card!. Anyone can catch a player sitting on one card who didn't call it. Getting caught costs two cards.
  • Empty your hand to win the round. You score the cards left in other hands: numbers at face value, action cards 20, wilds 50.
  • Each card color also has its own shape (circle, triangle, square, diamond), so colors are easy to tell apart.
