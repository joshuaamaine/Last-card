    How to play

    • Everyone gets 7 cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card by color, number or symbol. Wilds go on anything.
    • Can't play? Tap the deck to draw. If the new card fits, you can play it or keep it.
    • Skip jumps the next player. Reverse flips the order (with two players, you go again).
    • Stacking: hit with a draw card? Answer with one the same size or bigger (+2 on +2, +4 on +2 or +4, +10 on anything) and the pile moves on. Nothing to stack? You take the cards automatically.
    • Legendary (gold star): play it on its color and every card of that color in your hand goes with it.
    • Magic 7: swap hands with anyone you pick. Spin 0: everyone passes their hand along. Shuffle Hands: every card gets mixed and dealt back out.
    • Wild +10 turns up on about 1 in 100 cards. Jump-in: hold the exact same card as the top card? Play it any time.
    • You get 15 seconds a turn, then you draw. Reach 20 cards and you're out of the round.
    • Down to two cards on your turn? Tap Last card! If someone forgets, tap their face to catch them for two cards.
    • First to empty their hand wins and gets a medal. Everyone taps Ready for the next round.
